Privacy Policy for Shopify Flow Manager

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Shopify Flow Manager ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Shopify Flow Manager functions primarily as a client-side productivity tool designed to help merchants optimize catalog hierarchy, automate inventory sync, and streamline product SEO. We collect minimal operational data:

  • Shopify Store Information: When you execute automation workflows, store domain and administrative contact details are retrieved via authorized Shopify GraphQL Admin APIs solely to provide product updates and customer support.
  • API Tokens & Credentials: Your Shopify Admin API Access Token and third-party AI keys (Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter) are saved strictly inside your local browser via chrome.storage.local. These credentials never pass through external intermediary servers.
  • Product Data: Product titles, descriptions, tags, and inventory counts are transmitted directly from your browser to Shopify's official GraphQL endpoint and the designated AI provider you choose.

2. Third-Party Services

Depending on your configured settings, the extension may communicate with:

  • Shopify Admin API: To create collections, manage menus, and update product listings.
  • AI Providers (Google Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter): Only product metadata explicitly sent for optimization is processed in real time by the selected AI engine.
  • Internal Logging (Google Sheets API): Basic merchant interaction logs (store domain and action timestamp) are recorded to prevent abuse and deliver feature improvements.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your store data or customer records to any third parties or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. User Rights & Data Removal

You can remove all locally stored API keys and tokens at any time simply by clearing the input fields or uninstalling the Chrome Extension from your browser.
